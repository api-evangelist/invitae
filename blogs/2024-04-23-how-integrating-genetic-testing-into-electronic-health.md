EHR systems | Genetic testing Invitae explores the potential of EHR integration in simplifying genetic testing workflows In today’s digital healthcare landscape, electronic health records (EHRs) are the central hub for storing, managing, and accessing protected health information (PHI). Unlike electronic medical records (EMRs) focusing on patient care and information exchange within a single practice, EHRs cover a broader range of PHI between healthcare organizations, providers, and partners. Efficient EHR workflows are essential for streamlining clinical processes, enhancing care coordination
