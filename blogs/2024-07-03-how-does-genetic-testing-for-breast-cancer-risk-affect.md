Breast Cancer | Genetic Testing More research supports the benefits of genetic testing for breast cancer risk Genetic tests for inherited cancer risk look at gene changes that may directly inform healthcare decisions. They can help you consider risk-reducing measures like more frequent screenings. And for people who already have a cancer diagnosis, it can help inform treatment, including surgical strategy and the potential use of targeted therapies.
